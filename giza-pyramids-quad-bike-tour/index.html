Giza Pyramids Quad Bike Tour

A private quad bike ride in the desert near the Giza Pyramids, with hotel pickup and drop-off.

The Experience

This is a private quad bike ride in the open desert near the Giza Pyramids. You ride across sandy and rocky desert tracks with the pyramids on the horizon, stopping along the way for photographs. It suits travelers who want an active, outdoor break between sightseeing days or a different view of the pyramids after visiting the site itself.

Where the Ride Takes Place

The ride takes place in the desert area near the Giza Pyramids. It is not a tour of the archaeological site itself: the focus is the desert landscape and views of the pyramids from a distance. To see the pyramids and the Sphinx up close with a licensed Egyptologist, combine the ride with our Giza Pyramids & Grand Egyptian Museum tour.

What to Expect

Before you start, you receive a safety briefing and instructions on how to control the quad bike. No previous riding experience is required.

Because the ride is private, the timing can be discussed when you book, and it can be combined with other activities in Giza or Cairo. In the warmer months, early morning or late afternoon is usually more comfortable than the middle of the day. For more ideas, see our guide to things to do in Cairo, and read our Egypt travel safety tips before your trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear for the quad bike tour?

We recommend wearing comfortable, loose clothing and closed-toe shoes for the best riding experience. Sunglasses and a hat are also recommended to protect against the sun.

Do I need previous riding experience?

No previous experience is required. Before you start, you receive a safety briefing and instructions on how to control the quad bike.

Can we add sandboarding to the tour?

No, sandboarding is not included in the quad bike tour. However, we can arrange for a separate sandboarding activity if you're interested.
