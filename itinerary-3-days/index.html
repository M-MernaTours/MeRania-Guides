Recommended 3-Day Sequence

For a balanced pace without back-to-back long highway drives, we suggest:

  • Day 1: Giza Pyramids, Sphinx & Grand Egyptian Museum.
  • Day 2: Memphis, Saqqara & NMEC (Old Kingdom origins and Royal Mummies).
  • Day 3: Alexandria full-day coastal trip, or stay in town for Islamic & Coptic Cairo.
