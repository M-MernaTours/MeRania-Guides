10-Day Private Egypt Itinerary: Cairo, Luxor & Aswan

Designed for travelers who want the classic Cairo-to-Abu-Simbel route at a steadier pace. Compared with our 8-day schedule, the extra two days open up unhurried time in Aswan for Nubian culture and a calm departure morning.

Days 1–3: Pyramids, Royal Mummies & Old Cairo

  • Begin on the Giza Plateau with the Great Pyramids, Sphinx, and Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), pacing the galleries around your interests.
  • Explore the Old Kingdom necropolis at Saqqara and Memphis, then connect those kings to the Royal Mummies Hall at NMEC.
  • Walk Saladin's Citadel, historic mosques, and Coptic Old Cairo before flying south to Luxor.

Days 4–6: Luxor's Temples & Royal Tombs

  • Dedicate an unhurried afternoon to Karnak's hypostyle hall and Luxor Temple as the colonnades light up at dusk.
  • Cross the Nile early for the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut's mortuary temple, and the Colossi of Memnon with time for extra tombs.
  • Optional sunrise hot-air balloon flight, with time to rest by the Nile before continuing south to Aswan.

Days 7–9: Aswan, Nubian Culture & Abu Simbel

  • Take a motorboat to Philae Temple on Agilkia Island and visit the Aswan High Dam on a relaxed first day in the south.
  • Make the early-morning desert journey to Abu Simbel to explore the Great Temple of Ramses II and the Temple of Nefertari.
  • Enjoy an afternoon Nile felucca sail and Nubian village visit—time the tighter 8-day route cannot easily fit.

Day 10: Relaxed Departure

  • Enjoy a unhurried morning by the Nile before your private transfer to the airport for your flight back to Cairo or onward travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 10-day pace differ from the 8-day itinerary?

Both cover Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, and Abu Simbel, but the 10-day schedule gives you three full days in the south plus a dedicated departure day—so you have time for a relaxed Nile felucca sail and Nubian village visit instead of flying out right after Abu Simbel.

Can we swap a Cairo day for Fayoum or include a Nile Cruise?

Yes. Because this is a private itinerary, we can swap one Cairo day for a Fayoum Desert Safari or discuss optional Nile Cruise and domestic flight arrangements when planning your dates.
