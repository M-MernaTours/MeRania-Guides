A Nile Sailing Experience in Cairo

A felucca is a traditional wooden sailing boat that has been used on the Nile for centuries. A ride on one is a simple, relaxed way to see Cairo from the river: the boat moves with the wind, the city noise fades, and you can watch the riverbanks and the skyline drift past. The ride is arranged privately for your group.

What to Expect on a Felucca Ride

A felucca is a small open boat with a single large sail, so the pace is gentle and depends on the breeze. There is seating on deck, often under a canopy, and the captain handles the sail. It is not a dinner cruise or a multi-day Nile cruise: for a longer journey on the river between Luxor and Aswan, see our 4-day Nile cruise itinerary.

Many travelers choose the late afternoon, when it is cooler and the light softens towards sunset, but a daytime sail is also pleasant. Weather and wind vary, so timing is agreed when you book.

Where Can You Enjoy a Felucca or Boat Ride in Egypt?

Felucca and local boat rides can be arranged in several destinations. Each one is a separate arrangement with its own details:

  • Cairo and Giza: the focus of this page, an easy addition to a day of sightseeing in the capital.
  • Luxor: sailing on the Nile between the East and West Banks of ancient Thebes.
  • Aswan: traditional felucca sailing on the Nile in southern Egypt, also part of our 12-day Egypt itinerary.
  • Alexandria (occasionally): a local boat ride on the Mediterranean Sea near the Citadel of Qaitbay. This is a sea outing, not a Nile felucca ride, and it is not always available.

The type of boat, meeting point, duration, timing and availability are confirmed when you book.

A felucca ride is a peaceful way to end a day at the Giza Pyramids and Grand Egyptian Museum or in Islamic and Coptic Cairo. For more ideas, see our guide to things to do in Cairo, and read our Egypt travel safety tips before you travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if there is no wind?

A felucca sails with the wind, so conditions vary from day to day. When the breeze is very light, the captain may use a small motor or oars to keep the boat moving.

Is a felucca ride the same as a Nile cruise?

No. A felucca is a small traditional sailing boat for a short ride on the river, while a Nile cruise is a multi-day trip on a larger boat, usually between Luxor and Aswan.

Can I take a felucca ride outside Cairo?

Yes. Felucca and local boat rides can also be arranged in Luxor and Aswan, and occasionally on the Mediterranean near the Citadel of Qaitbay in Alexandria. Each destination is arranged separately.

When is the best time for a felucca ride?

Late afternoon is a popular choice, when it is cooler and the light softens towards sunset. Timing depends on the wind and your plans, and is agreed when you book.
