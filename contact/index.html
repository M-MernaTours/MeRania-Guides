What to Include in Your Inquiry

  • Your arrival and departure dates (or preferred tour days)
  • Group size (solo traveler, couple, family, or private group)
  • Destinations of interest (Cairo, Giza & GEM, Saqqara, Alexandria, Fayoum, Luxor, Aswan, or a Nile cruise)
  • Whether you need private guiding and transport only, or airport transfers as well
  • Your preferred guiding language
