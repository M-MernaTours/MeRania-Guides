Frequently Asked Questions

Can we spread the 4 tours over a week?

Yes. You do not have to take them on consecutive days—we can space your four selected tours across a 5- to 7-day stay to leave rest days between full-day drives.

Is airport pickup included in the 4-tour package?

Yes. For guests booking the 4-tour package, we include private airport pickup and drop-off as a complimentary bonus service.

Are entrance tickets included in the 4-tour package?

Not by default. Airport transfers are included, but entrance tickets are your choice: buy them yourself where permitted, or ask us to include them in your agreed quotation, which lists exactly which tickets are covered.

Can we request Merna or Rania for our tours?

Yes, subject to availability. If they are already booked, they assign a suitable licensed guide from their team, and guiding is available in nine languages, subject to availability.
