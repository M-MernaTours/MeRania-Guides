Meet Merna & Rania

We are Merna and Rania, two licensed Egyptian Egyptologist tour guides welcoming international travelers from around the world. Rania has over 10 years of guiding experience, while Merna has over 5 years. Every tour we arrange is private, flexible, and tailored to your pace—whether you are traveling solo, as a couple, with family, or in a private group.

Alongside us, a team of 32 licensed male and female guides covers destinations across Egypt, with guiding available in nine languages, subject to availability. We help you find a suitable licensed guide for your itinerary and travel dates.

Having licensed female guides often offers added comfort and ease for women travelers and families, while every guest enjoys clear communication, deep historical context, and genuine local insight.
