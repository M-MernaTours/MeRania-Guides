Booking Process & Tour Policies

Clear, straightforward policies designed for peace of mind—no deposit required, no cancellation fees, final confirmed pricing, and direct communication with your licensed Egyptologist guides.

01

Meet Merna & Rania – Your Licensed Egyptologist Guides

Merna and Rania are two licensed female Egyptian Egyptologist tour guides welcoming travelers from around the world. Rania has over 10 years of guiding experience, while Merna has over 5 years.

We personally oversee every inquiry and itinerary so you always know who you are planning with. Learn more on our About Us page.

02

A Professional Team Across Egypt

Alongside Merna and Rania, our wider team consists of 32 licensed male and female guides covering all governorates of Egypt.

Merna and Rania coordinate the team directly and assign the most suitable licensed guide based on your destination, itinerary, preferred language, and travel dates. Guiding is available in 9 languages:

Arabic English Chinese Italian Japanese French German Spanish Greek
03

Private & Personalized Tours

Every tour we arrange is 100% private and customizable—never merged with other travelers. Whether you are booking a single private day tour or a multi-day Egypt itinerary, your schedule, pickup time, and pace are shaped around your group.

04

Transparent Pricing – No Hidden Fees

Once your booking is confirmed, the agreed price is final—with no hidden fees or surprise add-ons.

If fuel prices, transportation costs, or official government fees increase after your booking is confirmed, we absorb those increases ourselves. They are never passed on to you.

05

No Deposit – Pay When You Meet Your Guide

No deposit or advance payment is required to confirm your tour.

  • Payment is collected directly when you meet your guide.
  • Accepted currencies: USD, EUR, and EGP.
  • Payment methods: cash or bank transfer.
06

Flexible Booking & Cancellation

Travel plans can change, so we keep our policy simple and fair:

  • No cancellation fees and no minimum cancellation notice period—simply inform us when you decide to cancel.
  • No additional change fees for itinerary or date adjustments, subject to practical availability.
07

Entrance Tickets – You Have the Choice

How entrance tickets are handled depends on your agreed tour quotation. You are welcome to discuss ticket arrangements with us before your tour and choose whichever option suits you best:

  • Purchase applicable entrance tickets independently through official platforms, with our guidance.
  • Or ask us to arrange your entrance tickets and add their exact cost to your tour total.

Your quotation will clearly explain which entrance tickets are included and which, if any, are payable separately.

08

Transportation & Traffic in Egypt

All private tours use modern, air-conditioned vehicles with experienced drivers. Because traffic in Cairo and major cities can vary by time of day, we plan realistic start times and routes to make the most of your day.

See our Private Transportation & Transfers page for vehicle and airport transfer options.

09

Guest Delays & Waiting Time

Flights, hotel check-ins, and travel days do not always run on the minute. We charge no additional waiting fees for guest delays—just keep us updated so we can adjust smoothly.

10

No Forced Shopping – Your Choice Always

We strictly uphold a no forced shopping policy. You will never be taken to unplanned workshops or pressured to buy anything. Shopping stops are included only if you specifically request them.

11

Tips & Gratuities – Entirely Optional

Tips and gratuities for your guide or driver are entirely optional. There is no fixed amount and no required percentage—any gesture is always up to you.

12

Official Security Instructions & Guest Safety

Your safety comes first. Our guides and drivers follow all official Egyptian security instructions, site regulations, and route restrictions at all times to ensure a smooth and secure journey.

13

Available 24/7 – We're Here to Help

We are available 24/7 for inquiries before and during your trip in Egypt. Reach us anytime on WhatsApp or phone at +20 102 954 5765, or by email at mmernaguide@mmernaguide.info. You can also read Guest Reviews from previous travelers.
