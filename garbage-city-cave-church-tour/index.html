Garbage City (Zabbaleen) & Cave Church Private Tour

Discover a side of Cairo few visitors see. Travel through Manshiyat Naser, the neighborhood known as Garbage City and home to the Zabbaleen community, then visit two churches carved into the Mokattam hills, including the remarkable Cave Church of Saint Simon the Tanner.

Your licensed guide shares the story of the community and the churches with care and respect, with private air-conditioned transport and hotel pickup in Cairo or Giza.

What You Will Experience

This short private tour combines a respectful introduction to one of Cairo's most distinctive communities with visits to two churches cut into the cliffs of the Mokattam hills.

The Zabbaleen Neighborhood of Manshiyat Naser

At the foot of the Mokattam hills, Manshiyat Naser is home to the Zabbaleen, a largely Coptic Christian community whose families have collected, sorted, and recycled much of Cairo's household waste for generations. Visitors often know the area as "Garbage City."

You travel through the neighborhood by private vehicle, usually for about 20–30 minutes on the way to the churches, while your guide explains the history of the community and its recycling work. If something catches your eye, your guide can stop briefly so you can step out and take a photo, where it is safe, practical, and respectful to residents—please always ask before photographing people. This is not a walking tour, and visits inside workshops or homes are not included.

The Cave Church of Saint Simon the Tanner

High above the neighborhood, the Cave Church of Saint Simon the Tanner was carved into the rock of the Mokattam hills, with tiered seating arranged like a vast open-air amphitheater beneath the cliff. The community began building churches here in the 1970s, and today the complex is an active place of worship as well as a remarkable sight for visitors.

The church is named after Saint Simon the Tanner, who, according to Coptic tradition, is associated with the miracle of the moving of the Mokattam mountain in the 10th century. Your guide explains this tradition and the meaning of the carvings and inscriptions in the rock, while making clear what is history and what is religious tradition.

A Second Rock-Hewn Church

Your visit continues to a second rock-hewn church within the Mokattam church complex, a quieter space with rows of wooden pews carved with crosses set beneath the natural rock. Seeing two different churches helps you appreciate how the community shaped the mountain into places of prayer.

Your Visit, Your Pace: The tour usually takes about 4–5 hours in total, including hotel pickup and return in Cairo or Giza. Because the churches are active places of worship, your guide may adjust the order or timing of visits around services, traffic, and your interests.

Garbage City & Cave Church Tour FAQ

Is this a walking tour of Garbage City?

No. You travel through the Zabbaleen neighborhood of Manshiyat Naser by private air-conditioned vehicle, usually for about 20–30 minutes on the way to the churches, while your guide explains the community and its recycling work. If you would like to step out briefly to take a photo, your guide can stop where it is safe, practical, and respectful to residents.

Do we visit recycling workshops or homes?

No. Visits inside workshops, workplaces, or private homes are not part of this tour. Your guide shares the story of the community and its recycling activities from the vehicle and during any brief stops.

Which churches do we visit?

You visit two churches in the Mokattam rock-church complex: the famous Cave Church of Saint Simon the Tanner, with its vast amphitheater-like seating and large carved cross, and a second rock-hewn church within the same complex.

Is there an entrance fee for the churches?

Entry to both churches is free of charge at the time of writing. Site rules and visitor access are set by the churches and may change, and any donation is entirely your own choice.

How long does the tour take?

The tour usually takes about 4–5 hours in total, including hotel pickup in Cairo or Giza, the drive through the neighborhood, the church visits, and your return. Timing depends on traffic and your pace.

What should we wear, and can we take photos?

These are active places of worship, so modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees is recommended. Please follow any photography rules posted at the churches, and always ask before photographing local residents.

Can Merna or Rania guide us personally?

Yes, you can request Merna or Rania personally, subject to availability. Otherwise, they assign a suitable licensed guide from their team of 32 male and female guides, with guiding available in nine languages, subject to availability.

Do I need to pay a deposit to reserve this tour?

No deposit is required to reserve your private tour. Payment is made in cash when meeting your guide or by bank transfer in USD, EUR, or EGP. There are no cancellation penalties and no minimum notice period.
