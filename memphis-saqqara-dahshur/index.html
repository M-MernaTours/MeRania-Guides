Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur Private Day Tour

Step beyond the famous Pyramids of Giza and discover where Egypt's pyramid-building story began. On this private day tour, explore the ancient capital of Memphis, the Step Pyramid of Djoser at Saqqara, and the Bent and Red Pyramids at Dahshur.

Travel at your own pace with a licensed Egyptologist guide, comfortable private transportation, and a flexible itinerary designed around your interests.

Duration Approx. 7–9 hours including pickup & drop-off
Tour Type 100% Private — No Shared Groups
Pickup Cairo & Giza hotels (distant areas by arrangement)

Discover Three Remarkable Ancient Sites

Memphis — Egypt's Ancient Capital

Begin your journey into the early history of ancient Egypt at Memphis, one of the country's most important ancient capitals. Discover the colossal limestone statue of Ramses II, admire the Alabaster Sphinx, and explore the surviving archaeological treasures of the Memphis Open-Air Museum.

Your licensed Egyptologist guide explains why Memphis became such a vital political and religious center and how its story connects directly to the nearby pyramid fields.

Saqqara — The Birthplace of Pyramid Architecture

Travel to Saqqara, home to the extraordinary Step Pyramid of Djoser, one of the earliest monumental stone structures in Egypt. Explore the funerary complex and learn about Imhotep, the brilliant architect associated with this revolutionary achievement.

Your visit also includes the Pyramid of Unas, renowned for its carved Pyramid Texts, subject to official site access. If you would like to explore further, ask your guide about an optional visit to selected decorated tombs at Saqqara.

Dahshur — The Bent Pyramid & Red Pyramid

Continue through pyramid-building history at Dahshur, where ancient Egyptian architects experimented with bold new stone designs. Discover the distinctive angled profile of the Bent Pyramid and the smooth-sided engineering of the Red Pyramid, both associated with King Sneferu.

Standard Dahshur admission allows optional interior visits to both the Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid, subject to official site access and your physical suitability. Interior entry is completely optional, and your guide will explain the steep, narrow passages beforehand so you can decide comfortably.

Flexible Timing & Route Order: Pickup times are flexible, and the order of all three stops can be adjusted around your hotel location, preferences, and traffic. Because official archaeological site closing hours vary by site and season, your guide will plan the sequence so you enjoy comfortable time at Memphis, Saqqara, and Dahshur.
