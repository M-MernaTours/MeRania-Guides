8-Day Private Egypt Tour: Cairo, Luxor & Aswan

A focused, fast-paced route for travelers with one week in Egypt. You cover Cairo's pyramids and museums first, then fly south to Luxor and continue overland to Aswan, with licensed Egyptologist guides and private air-conditioned transport throughout.

Your 8-Day Route, Day by Day

Days 1–3: Giza, Saqqara & Historic Cairo

  • Day 1 covers the Giza Plateau, the Great Sphinx, and the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) while everything is close together on the Nile's west bank at Giza.
  • Day 2 explores Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur—ancient Memphis and the colossal statue of Ramses II, Djoser's Step Pyramid at Saqqara, and the Bent and Red Pyramids at Dahshur.
  • Day 3 focuses on medieval and Coptic Cairo—Saladin's Citadel, historic mosques and churches, and Old Cairo's lanes—before an evening flight to Luxor.

Days 4–6: Luxor East & West Banks

  • Explore the East Bank's monumental Karnak Temple complex and Luxor Temple, timed for late afternoon light when the heat eases.
  • Cross to the West Bank early for the Valley of the Kings, the Temple of Hatshepsut, and the Colossi of Memnon before peak tour-bus hours.
  • Optional sunrise hot-air balloon flight over the West Bank, plus overland transfer south to Aswan on Day 6.

Days 7–8: Aswan, Abu Simbel & Departure

  • Visit Philae Temple by motorboat on Agilkia Island and see the Aswan High Dam, followed by a quiet afternoon felucca sail on the Nile.
  • Early-morning private drive to Abu Simbel to walk the twin rock-cut temples of Ramses II and Nefertari with your guide.
  • Return to Aswan for your departure flight back to Cairo or your onward connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we include a Nile Cruise in this 8-day trip?

The standard 8-day outline follows a land-and-flight route between Cairo, Luxor, and Aswan. If you prefer to sail part of Upper Egypt on a Nile Cruise, we can discuss optional cruise arrangements and adjust the schedule to fit your dates.

Do you help with domestic flights?

Yes. Because 8 days is a fast-paced route, we recommend flying from Cairo to Luxor and returning from Aswan to maximize sightseeing time. We can coordinate flight timing and airport transfers as part of your plan.

Will Merna or Rania guide us for all 8 days?

You can request Merna or Rania personally, subject to availability. Otherwise, they assign the most suitable guides from their team of 32 licensed male and female guides, and guiding is available in nine languages, subject to availability.
