Two Museums, Two Different Experiences

Cairo has three major museums of ancient Egypt, and they are easy to confuse. This tour visits the historic Egyptian Museum in Tahrir Square and the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization in Fustat. It does not include the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) near the Giza Pyramids, which you can explore on our separate Giza Pyramids & GEM tour.

The Egyptian Museum, Tahrir Square

Opened in 1902 in a landmark building designed by the French architect Marcel Dourgnon, the Egyptian Museum on Tahrir Square has been the home of Egyptology in Cairo for more than a century. Its galleries hold an enormous collection of statues, coffins, jewelry, papyri, and everyday objects spanning thousands of years of Egyptian history.

Some famous collections, including much of Tutankhamun's treasure, have moved to the Grand Egyptian Museum, and displays continue to change as objects are transferred. Your guide focuses on what is on view on the day, helping you find the highlights and understand the stories behind them in this historic setting.

The National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC), Fustat

Overlooking the historic district of Fustat in Old Cairo, about seven kilometers from Tahrir, NMEC tells the story of Egyptian civilization across its history, from prehistoric times to later eras. Its modern galleries help you see how life, beliefs, crafts, and society developed over thousands of years.

The museum is best known for its Royal Mummies Hall. In April 2021, the royal mummies of 18 kings and 4 queens were moved here from the Tahrir museum in the ceremonial Pharaohs' Golden Parade, and the hall was designed to evoke the Valley of the Kings where many of them were found. Visits follow the museum's own rules for this hall, which your guide will explain beforehand.

Your Day, Your Pace: The tour usually takes about 6–8 hours including hotel pickup and return. Pickup time and the order of the two museums can be adjusted around your hotel location, traffic, and interests, and your guide plans the visit around official museum opening hours, which can change.

Egyptian Museum & NMEC Tour FAQ

Is this tour the same as a Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) tour?

No. This tour visits the historic Egyptian Museum in Tahrir Square and the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC) in Fustat. The Grand Egyptian Museum near the Giza Pyramids is a different museum and is covered by our separate Giza Pyramids & GEM tour.

How long does the tour take?

The tour usually takes about 6–8 hours, including hotel pickup and return in Cairo or Giza. The exact timing depends on traffic, your pace, and official museum opening hours.

Will we see the royal mummies?

Yes. The visit to NMEC includes the Royal Mummies Hall, subject to the museum's admission rules and access on the day. Your guide will explain the hall's visitor rules before you enter.

Can you guarantee that a specific artifact will be on display?

We cannot guarantee specific objects, because museum displays change as artifacts are transferred, restored, or loaned. Your guide will focus on the highlights that are on view during your visit.

How are entrance tickets handled?

Each museum has its own official entrance ticket. You can buy your tickets yourself where permitted or have them included in your personalized quotation, which will always state clearly which tickets are included.

Can Merna or Rania guide us personally?

Yes, you can request Merna or Rania personally, subject to availability. Otherwise, they assign a suitable licensed tour guide from their team of 32 male and female guides, with guiding available in nine languages, subject to availability.

Do I need to pay a deposit to reserve this tour?

No deposit is required to reserve your private tour. Payment is made in cash when meeting your guide or by bank transfer in USD, EUR, or EGP. There are no cancellation penalties and no minimum notice period.
