Giza Pyramids & Grand Egyptian Museum: Visitor Guide

Practical tips on tickets, timing, and making the most of your visit to Cairo's greatest treasures.

The Eternal Giza Pyramids

Visiting the Giza Plateau is a bucket-list dream. Many travelers wonder about the quality of private tours; you can read our guest experiences and reviews here to see what to expect.

Knowing a few local tips is still key to avoiding the crowds and the midday heat. For sweeping desert views and photos, the panoramic viewpoint on the Giza Plateau is a popular stop. To explore the plateau and the museum with a licensed Egyptologist, see our private Giza Pyramids & GEM tour.

Want to go beyond Giza? Many of our guests extend their pyramid journey with our Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur Tour, home to Egypt's earliest stone pyramids and the ancient capital of Memphis.

Giza Plateau Tickets

Entrance fees for the Giza Plateau can change, and separate official tickets apply to optional special entries, such as the inside of the Great Pyramid. Please check current prices and ticket categories through the official booking channels before you visit.

* Discounted ticket categories, such as student tickets, may require valid ID.

The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)

The GEM combines ancient heritage with striking modern architecture. From the Hanging Obelisk to the Grand Staircase, every corner is a masterpiece of modern architecture meeting ancient heritage.

Grand Egyptian Museum Tickets

The Grand Egyptian Museum sets its own ticket prices and categories, which can change. Please check current fees and booking options through the museum's official booking channels before you visit.

Essential Travel Notes:

  • Payment Methods: Ticketing and accepted payment methods at the Pyramids and GEM can change, so please confirm the current options through the official sources before your visit.
  • Pre-Booking: We highly recommend booking your GEM tickets online through the official portal to ensure availability during peak seasons.
  • The Value of a Guide: Having a licensed guide helps manage the logistics and ensures you don't miss the hidden stories behind the artifacts. Our clients often highlight this in their latest reviews.
