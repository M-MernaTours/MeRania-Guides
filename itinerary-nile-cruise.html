4-Day / 3-Night Private Nile Cruise (Luxor to Aswan)

Sail Upper Egypt from Luxor to Aswan on a 5-star full-board cruise ship while keeping every temple visit and shore transfer 100% private with your own licensed Egyptologist guide.

4-Day / 3-Night Sailing Schedule (Luxor to Aswan)

Day 1: Luxor East Bank & Embarkation

Arrive in Luxor and board your cruise ship before lunch. In the afternoon, explore Karnak Temple—the largest religious complex built in ancient Egypt—followed by Luxor Temple as the evening lights come on. Overnight on board in Luxor.

Day 2: Luxor West Bank & Esna Lock

Cross to the West Bank early for the Valley of the Kings, the Temple of Hatshepsut, and the Colossi of Memnon. Return to the ship for lunch and begin sailing south toward Edfu, passing through the Esna Lock along the way.

Day 3: Edfu & Kom Ombo Temples

Visit the remarkably intact Ptolemaic Temple of Horus at Edfu in the morning. Continue sailing south to Kom Ombo to explore the riverside double temple dedicated to Sobek and Haroeris before sailing on to Aswan.

Day 4: Aswan, Philae Temple & Disembarkation

After breakfast, visit the island sanctuary of Philae Temple by motorboat and see the Aswan High Dam (with an optional early-morning excursion to Abu Simbel). Disembark and transfer privately to the airport or your hotel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we add airport transfers to the package?

Yes. We coordinate private door-to-door transfers from Luxor or Aswan airports (or your hotel) straight to your cruise ship on embarkation and disembarkation days.

Are all excursions private?

Yes. While the cruise vessel itself is shared with other passengers, all of your guided sightseeing excursions in Luxor, Edfu, Kom Ombo, and Aswan are 100% private with Merna or Rania.
