Cairo and Giza Experiences: A Visual Guide

From ancient wonders to bazaar evenings and quiet hours on the Nile, here is what Cairo and Giza have to offer.

Cairo and neighboring Giza hold some of the most famous sights on earth, but a visit here is about more than monuments. This visual guide brings together ten experiences our guests enjoy, each shown with a real photograph from our tours, so you can picture your days and decide what to combine.

For a shorter overview of activities and practical planning advice, see our things to do in Cairo page.

Ancient Wonders

Most visits begin with the monuments and museums that made Cairo and Giza famous.

The Pyramids and the Sphinx

Stand beside the Great Pyramid of Khufu and the Great Sphinx on the Giza Plateau, the only surviving wonder of the ancient world. A licensed Egyptologist brings the history of the pyramid builders to life.

Giza Pyramids & GEM tour

The Grand Egyptian Museum

Close to the pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum displays treasures from thousands of years of Egyptian history, with a colossal statue of Ramesses II greeting visitors inside.

Plan a GEM visit

Saqqara, Memphis and Dahshur

South of Giza, Saqqara's Step Pyramid of Djoser, the ancient capital of Memphis and the Bent and Red Pyramids at Dahshur show how pyramid building began.

Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur tour

The Egyptian Museum and NMEC

In central Cairo, the historic Egyptian Museum in Tahrir pairs well with the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization and its Royal Mummies Hall.

Egyptian Museum & NMEC tour

Historic Cairo

Centuries of Islamic and Christian heritage sit within a short drive of each other.

Islamic Cairo and the Citadel

The Citadel of Salah El-Din and its Mosque of Muhammad Ali overlook the city, while the Sultan Hassan Mosque below is one of the great monuments of medieval Cairo.

Islamic & Coptic Cairo tour

Coptic Cairo

The narrow lanes of Coptic Cairo lead to the Hanging Church and the Church of St. Sergius and Bacchus (Abu Serga), among the city's oldest Christian sites.

Explore Islamic & Coptic Cairo

Khan El Khalili Bazaar

Lantern shops, craft workshops and traditional coffee houses fill the historic alleys of Khan El Khalili and nearby Al-Muizz Street, especially lively in the evening.

Khan El Khalili walking tour

Outdoor and Relaxed Experiences

Optional activities that add variety between sightseeing days. Each is arranged separately and confirmed when you book.

Camel Riding

A camel ride is a classic way to enjoy the desert setting of the Giza Pyramids area, and rides can also be arranged in Saqqara.

Camel ride at the pyramids

Quad Biking Near the Pyramids

For something more active, a private quad bike ride crosses the desert near the Giza Pyramids, with the pyramids on the horizon. It does not enter the archaeological site.

Quad bike ride near the pyramids

Evenings on the Nile

End a busy day on the river. A traditional felucca sails with the wind, offering a slower, quieter view of the city from the water.

Nile felucca ride in Cairo

Combining Experiences

A Giza Day

The Pyramids and the Sphinx with the Grand Egyptian Museum, with a camel or quad bike ride added if you would like.

A Historic Cairo Day

Islamic and Coptic Cairo by day, then Khan El Khalili in the evening, when the bazaar is at its liveliest.

A Slower Day

A museum visit in central Cairo followed by a felucca ride on the Nile in the late afternoon.

Practical Tips

  • Plan around the heat: in the warmer months, open-air sites, camel rides and quad biking are more comfortable early in the morning or later in the afternoon.
  • Give Khan El Khalili its own time: the bazaar is best enjoyed at a relaxed pace rather than squeezed in at the end of a long day.
  • Tickets and activities are separate: optional activities do not automatically include admission to archaeological sites or museums.
  • Carry water and small notes: the sun is strong at Giza, and small Egyptian pound notes are useful for tips and small purchases.
  • Before you travel: read our Egypt travel safety tips for advice on documents, money and local customs.
