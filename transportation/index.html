Transportation or a Guided Tour?

A transportation booking includes a private vehicle and driver. It does not include a licensed tour guide unless one is arranged separately. If you would like expert guiding at the sites, we can add a licensed guide from our team of 32 licensed male and female guides, with nine languages subject to availability, or you can choose one of our private day tours or multi-day itineraries, which combine guiding and transport. You can also browse all our tours and services.

How Our Private Transportation Works

Every transfer is private to your group, with no shared rides. Our vehicles include HiAce vans and private cars, matched to your group size and luggage.

Within Cairo & Giza

Our full-day private driver service within Cairo and Giza typically covers 8–10 hours. The agreed price includes fuel, road tolls, and parking, with no additional waiting fees for guest delays under our local service policy. Because traffic in Cairo can vary by time of day, we plan realistic pickup times and routes with you in advance.

Intercity Transfers Across Egypt

Transfers between Egyptian cities and governorates are arranged individually. Prices, inclusions, and schedules depend on the route and travel requirements, and the Cairo and Giza terms above do not automatically apply. Some journeys involving foreign visitors may require additional security arrangements or permits. We will clarify the applicable arrangements before confirming your booking.

How to Request a Quote

Rates depend on your route, vehicle size, number of passengers, and schedule, so we prepare a personalized quotation that states what is included. Please send us:

  • Your pickup point and destination, or the airport and your hotel, including any planned stops on intercity routes
  • Your travel date and time, including your flight number for airport transfers
  • The number of passengers and pieces of luggage
  • Whether you would also like a licensed guide

Once your booking is confirmed, the agreed price is final, with no hidden fees. No deposit is required under our booking terms, and payment is accepted in EGP, USD, or EUR, by cash or bank transfer. Please read our Booking Process & Policies for full terms.
