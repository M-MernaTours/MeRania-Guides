Things to Do in Cairo & Giza

Cairo and neighboring Giza combine ancient monuments, major museums, historic neighborhoods and relaxed outdoor experiences. Here is how to choose what suits your trip.

Camel Ride at the Pyramids

A classic way to enjoy the desert setting of the Giza Pyramids. The ride is arranged privately and can be combined with a guided visit to the pyramids.

Khan El Khalili Bazaar Tour

A guided walk through the historic alleys of one of Cairo's best-known markets, with its crafts, workshops and traditional coffee houses.

View Experience

Nile Felucca Ride

A relaxed private sail on a traditional wooden felucca on the Nile in Cairo. The hour before sunset is a popular time to go.

A private quad bike ride in the desert near the Giza Pyramids, with views of the pyramids on the horizon. No riding experience is needed.

Historic Sights in Cairo and Giza

Most visitors build their days around the great historical sites. These are usually visited with a licensed Egyptologist guide, and entrance tickets are separate from optional activities.

Giza Pyramids & the Grand Egyptian Museum

The Pyramids of Giza and the Great Sphinx on the Giza Plateau, combined with the Grand Egyptian Museum nearby.

Giza Pyramids & GEM tour

Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur

South of Giza: the ancient capital of Memphis, the Step Pyramid at Saqqara and the Bent and Red Pyramids at Dahshur.

Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur tour

Islamic & Coptic Cairo

The Citadel of Salah El-Din and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, the Sultan Hassan Mosque and the historic churches of Coptic Cairo.

Islamic & Coptic Cairo tour

The Egyptian Museum & NMEC

The historic Egyptian Museum in Tahrir and the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization, home to the Royal Mummies Hall.

Egyptian Museum & NMEC tour

Garbage City & the Cave Church

The Zabbaleen community of Mokattam and its rock-cut churches, including the Cave Church of St Simon.

Garbage City & Cave Church tour

Planning Your Days in Cairo

  • Cairo and Giza are different areas: the Pyramids and the Grand Egyptian Museum are in Giza, while Islamic and Coptic Cairo, Khan El Khalili and the Egyptian Museum are in Cairo itself. Grouping nearby sights on the same day saves time in traffic.
  • Historic sites and activities are booked separately: a camel ride or quad bike ride does not automatically include admission to the archaeological site, and site entrance tickets are separate from optional activities.
  • Plan around the heat: in the warmer months, open-air sites and desert activities are more comfortable early in the morning or later in the afternoon.
  • Leave time to slow down: a felucca ride or an evening in Khan El Khalili is a relaxing contrast to a full day of monuments.
  • Prepare for the trip: our Egypt travel safety tips cover documents, money, tipping and local customs.
