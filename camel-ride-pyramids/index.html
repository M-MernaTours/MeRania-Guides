Camel Ride at the Giza Pyramids

A classic Giza experience, arranged privately for your group.

The Experience

A camel ride is one of the most traditional ways to enjoy the desert setting of the Giza Pyramids. The camels are led by local camel handlers, and the ride is arranged privately for your group. It is a relaxed activity focused on the scenery and on photographs, rather than a tour of the monuments themselves.

Camel Ride or Guided Pyramids Tour?

The camel ride does not include a guided tour of the pyramids or the Pyramids area entrance ticket. To explore the pyramids, the Sphinx and the Grand Egyptian Museum with a licensed Egyptologist, book our Giza Pyramids & GEM tour; a camel ride can be added on the same day when you arrange it in advance. If you prefer a more active ride, see our quad bike ride near the pyramids.

Where Can You Enjoy a Camel Ride in Egypt?

Camel rides can be arranged in three destinations. Each is a separate arrangement rather than one combined ride:

  • Giza Pyramids area: the classic setting, close to Cairo and the focus of this page.
  • Saqqara: south of Giza, home to the Step Pyramid of Djoser. A ride here fits well with our Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur tour.
  • Luxor: in southern Egypt, for travelers spending time among the temples and tombs of ancient Thebes. See our private day tours for Luxor options.

Availability, meeting arrangements and the details of each ride are confirmed when you book.

Comfort and Responsible Riding

Wear comfortable trousers and closed shoes, and bring a hat, sunglasses and sun protection. Follow the handler's instructions, hold on when the camel stands up or kneels down, and let the handler know if you would like to slow down or stop.

Please also pay attention to the animals. If you are uncomfortable with an animal's condition or how it is being treated, you are free to decline the ride and let us know. For more practical advice, read our Egypt travel safety tips or explore other things to do in Cairo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need riding experience?

No previous experience is needed. The camel is led by a handler, who helps you mount and dismount; the camel kneels down for both.

Does the camel ride include a guided tour of the pyramids?

No. The camel ride is a separate activity and does not include a guided tour of the monuments or the Pyramids area entrance ticket. It can be combined with our Giza Pyramids and Grand Egyptian Museum tour with a licensed Egyptologist.

Can I ride a camel in Saqqara or Luxor?

Yes. Besides the Giza Pyramids area, camel rides can also be arranged in Saqqara and in Luxor. Each location is arranged separately, and availability and meeting details are confirmed when you book.

What is the best time for a camel ride?

In warm weather, early morning or late afternoon is usually more comfortable, and the softer light is good for photos.
