12-Day Private Egypt Itinerary: Cairo, Alexandria & the Nile

Our most complete Egypt route. Twelve days lets you pair Cairo's Old Kingdom and medieval sites with a full-day coastal trip to Alexandria, followed by Luxor, a Luxor-to-Aswan Nile Cruise via Edfu and Kom Ombo, and Abu Simbel.

Days 1–4: Cairo, Giza & Mediterranean Alexandria

  • Start with Giza's Great Pyramids, the Sphinx, and the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM).
  • Trace early pyramid engineering at Saqqara and Memphis, paired with the Royal Mummies Hall at NMEC.
  • Take a full-day coastal drive to Alexandria for Qaitbay Citadel, the Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa, and the modern Bibliotheca Alexandrina.
  • Spend your fourth day walking Saladin's Citadel and the historic churches and lanes of Old Cairo (or swap a Cairo day for Fayoum on request).

Days 5–6: Luxor East & West Banks

  • Fly south to Luxor and explore the East Bank—Karnak Temple and Luxor Temple—at a relaxed pace.
  • Cross to the West Bank for the Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut's mortuary temple, and the Colossi of Memnon with unhurried Egyptologist context inside the tombs.
  • If you are boarding a Luxor-to-Aswan Nile Cruise, these East and West Bank visits are coordinated once with your embarkation schedule so nothing is repeated.

Days 7–9: Sailing South via Edfu & Kom Ombo

  • Cruise south along the Nile from Luxor toward Aswan, with private shore guiding at every stop.
  • Visit the remarkably intact Temple of Horus at Edfu and the riverside twin temple of Sobek and Haroeris at Kom Ombo.
  • Enjoy traditional Nile felucca sailing and Nubian cultural time on arrival in Aswan.

Days 10–12: Aswan, Abu Simbel & Departure

  • Visit Philae Temple on Agilkia Island and the Aswan High Dam with your private guide.
  • Make the early-morning desert excursion to the twin temples of Abu Simbel before returning to Aswan.
  • Spend a calm final morning by the river before your private airport transfer for departure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the Luxor days and the Nile Cruise fit together without repeating sites?

When you sail from Luxor to Aswan, your East and West Bank visits are scheduled once at the start of the Luxor stay so nothing is duplicated. Exact embarkation and sailing days are aligned with your preferred ship schedule when we plan your dates.

Are our shore excursions private if we take a Nile Cruise?

Yes. While the cruise vessel itself is shared, all of your guiding, ground transfers, and temple visits in Cairo, Alexandria, Luxor, Edfu, Kom Ombo, Aswan, and Abu Simbel remain 100% private.
