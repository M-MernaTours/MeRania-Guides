Private Fayoum Day Tour: Wadi El-Hitan & Desert Safari

Swap the city for desert lakes, golden dunes, and the whale fossils of Wadi El-Hitan on a private day trip from Cairo.

Your Day in Fayoum

Fayoum is one of the easiest ways to see a completely different side of Egypt in a single day. After a private pickup from your hotel in Cairo or Giza, you drive southwest into the desert, usually about two hours each way depending on traffic. For guests who have already explored the Giza Pyramids, it is a striking contrast to the stone monuments of the Nile Valley.

Your day includes the protected area of Wadi El-Rayan, known for its desert lakes linked by small waterfalls and surrounded by sand dunes, and a private 4x4 desert safari to Wadi El-Hitan (Valley of the Whales). Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2005, Wadi El-Hitan preserves fossils of early whales, around 40 million years old, that show how these animals evolved from land-dwelling mammals into ocean-going whales.

In the desert, the safari is led by a local Bedouin guide who knows the area, travelling by 4x4 vehicle with an experienced driver. A licensed Egyptologist from our team can accompany your journey according to your agreed arrangement, and the off-road sections can be kept smooth and relaxed or made more adventurous, according to your preference. Sandboarding on the dunes can be added on request, where conditions allow. To learn more about the landscapes before you go, explore our Fayoum desert safari guide.

Included

  • A licensed Egyptologist guide accompanying your journey, as agreed in your quotation
  • Private 4x4 safari vehicle and driver, with a local Bedouin guide for the desert sections
  • Private transport with hotel pickup in Cairo or Giza

Fayoum Day Tour FAQ

How long is the Fayoum day tour from Cairo?

The full day takes approximately 10 hours, including a drive of about two hours each way from Cairo or Giza, depending on traffic.

Is the 4x4 desert safari included?

Yes. The private tour includes a 4x4 safari vehicle and driver for the desert sections, including the drive to Wadi El-Hitan, with a local Bedouin guide leading the safari.

Is the Fayoum safari suitable for older travelers?

Yes. The safari takes place in a 4x4 vehicle, and we can keep the dune driving as smooth or as adventurous as you prefer.

Can we add sandboarding?

Yes. Because the tour is private, sandboarding on the dunes can be added on request, where conditions allow.

How are Wadi El-Hitan entrance fees handled?

Entrance and protected-area fees for Wadi El-Hitan and Wadi El-Rayan are handled according to official site procedures. They are not automatically included; any fees included in your tour will be clearly stated in your agreed personalized quotation.
