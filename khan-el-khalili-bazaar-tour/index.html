A Living Historic Market

Khan El Khalili has been a center of trade in Cairo since the 14th century. Its narrow lanes, stone archways and small workshops are filled with brass lanterns, copperware, textiles, spices and handmade crafts, and the surrounding streets lead into the heart of historic Islamic Cairo.

On a private walk, your guide helps you find your way through the maze of alleys, shares the history of the area and its architecture, and suggests good places to stop for photographs or a drink. You set the pace, and shopping is always your choice.

Why Visit in the Evening

As the day cools down, the market lights come on and the streets fill with families, locals and visitors. Lanterns glow in the shop fronts, the cafés spill out into the lanes, and the whole quarter takes on a lively, festive atmosphere.

Stopping at a traditional coffee house for tea or a soft drink is part of the experience for many travelers. Refreshments are not included and are paid for on the spot, so you can choose what you like.

The Historic Surroundings

The bazaar sits among some of the finest monuments of medieval Cairo. Nearby streets are lined with historic mosques, minarets and old merchant buildings, which look especially beautiful when lit in the evening.

An evening walk focuses on the market and its streets; entry to religious sites at night is not part of the experience. To explore Cairo's mosques, the Citadel and the churches of Coptic Cairo in depth, join our Islamic & Coptic Cairo tour during the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Khan El Khalili?

We recommend the evening, from about 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM, when the market lights are on and the streets and cafés are lively. Pickup and drop-off travel time is not included in these three hours. Other times can be arranged when feasible.

Can I visit Khan El Khalili after another tour on the same day?

Yes. A private Khan El Khalili evening can be arranged after another daytime tour, such as the Giza Pyramids or the Grand Egyptian Museum, with timing confirmed when you book. It is arranged separately and is not automatically included in another tour.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. Shopping is entirely your choice. If you would like to buy something, your guide can help you understand fair prices and bargaining.

Is transport included?

Transport is available on request. Pickup and drop-off are arranged separately, and the travel time depends on where you are staying.
