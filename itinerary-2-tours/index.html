Egyptologist Recommendation

With only two days in Cairo, we recommend pairing Day 1: Giza Pyramids & GEM with Day 2: Memphis, Saqqara & Dahshur. Keeping both days inside Greater Cairo saves hours of highway driving and gives you the full chronological story of pyramid building. If you prefer more historical variety inside Cairo, pair Giza with Islamic & Coptic Cairo. You can also choose Alexandria or Fayoum for Day 2 if you are comfortable with a full-day trip (often around 10–12 hours including driving and visits). We do not recommend combining both Alexandria and Fayoum in a 2-day stay due to the long distances involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we customize which two tours we take?

Yes. You can choose any two of our seven private day tours from Cairo and set a starting time that fits your flight or hotel schedule—ideal for 48-hour layovers or short weekend stays.

Are entrance tickets included in the 2-tour package?

Not automatically. You can buy official tickets yourself where the site allows it, or ask us to include them in your personalized quotation. Your quote will list exactly which tickets are covered.

Do we need to pay a deposit to book?

No deposit is required, and you can cancel without penalty. Payment is made when you meet your guide, in EGP, USD, or EUR, in cash or by bank transfer.
