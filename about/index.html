Our Approach to Private Tours

How we like to work with every traveler who plans a trip with us.

Private Experiences

Our tours are private, planned for you and your own group rather than shared with strangers.

Your Own Pace

Spend longer where you are most interested and move on when you are ready. The day follows your rhythm.

Licensed Guiding

Your tour is led by a licensed tour guide who explains the history and culture behind each place you visit.

Clear Arrangements

We agree on the plan, timing and details with you before the tour, so you know what to expect. See our booking process.

No Forced Shopping

Shopping is always your choice. We will not push you into shops or purchases you did not ask for.

Honest Communication

We give you straightforward information and answer your questions openly, before and during your trip.
