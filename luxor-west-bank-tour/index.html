What You Will See on Luxor's West Bank

The West Bank was ancient Thebes' great city of the dead and is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Ancient Thebes with its Necropolis. Royal tombs, mortuary temples, and colossal statues reveal how Egypt's pharaohs prepared for eternity.

Valley of the Kings — Royal Tombs of the New Kingdom

For roughly five centuries, New Kingdom pharaohs were buried in rock-cut tombs hidden in this desert valley. Their painted corridors and burial chambers are covered with religious texts and scenes meant to guide the king safely through the afterlife.

The standard Valley of the Kings ticket covers a limited number of the tombs open on the day, and the list of open tombs changes periodically for conservation. Some tombs, including Tutankhamun's tomb (KV62)—the only royal tomb in the valley discovered largely intact—require separate admission under current official rules. Your guide can help you decide which are worth adding.

Temple of Hatshepsut — Deir el-Bahari

Set against the cliffs of Deir el-Bahari, the mortuary temple of Queen Hatshepsut rises in three terraces joined by long ramps. Built for one of Egypt's few female pharaohs and dedicated to the god Amun, its design is traditionally credited to her official Senenmut.

Look for the Osiride statues of the queen on the upper terrace and the famous reliefs of her trading expedition to the land of Punt.

Colossi of Memnon — Amenhotep III

Two enormous seated statues of Amenhotep III, around 18 meters tall, once guarded the entrance to his mortuary temple, which has now largely disappeared. Greek travelers later linked them with the legendary hero Memnon, and the name has stayed ever since.

Under current official rules, Tutankhamun's tomb (KV62) requires separate admission in addition to the standard Valley of the Kings ticket, subject to availability. Visiting it is optional, and you can buy the ticket yourself where permitted or ask us to include it in your personalized quotation.

How are entrance tickets handled for this tour?

You can choose either to buy your entrance tickets yourself or to have them included in your personalized tour quotation. Your confirmed quotation will always state clearly which tickets, including any additional tombs, are included.
